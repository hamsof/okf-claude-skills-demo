Sale by Product

salesByProduct(invoices) rolls every invoice line up by SKU into one row per product:

Field Meaning
name product name
qty total units sold across all invoices
revenue price * qty summed

Rows are returned sorted by revenue descending. The aggregation walks invoice.items — so it depends entirely on the invoice shape from checkout.

flowchart LR
    A["Invoice[]"] --> B["flatten<br/>invoice.items"]
    B --> C["group by SKU<br/>sum qty + revenue"]
    C --> D["sort by<br/>revenue desc"]
    D --> E["rows:<br/>{name, qty, revenue}"]
